# Site surfaces

Mode: Operate for directory; Read for explanatory pages. Visitors find links first, then learn how to adopt the project. No dependency, server, database, or frontend framework.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A precise, navigable bookmark register; the directory is the product, not an afterthought beneath a sales hero.

OWN-WORLD: Cool porcelain and deep ink, with a single clear blue reserved for links and actions. Rows are ruled like an index, with generous alignment and restrained rounded controls. Dark mode inverts the paper without losing the hierarchy.

STORY: See available codes and destinations immediately; follow one or move to a concise guide, mechanism explanation, or project rationale.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact site header, a strong editorial title and one-sentence orientation above a ruled list of real links. An adjacent narrow note explains how links are published; mobile stacks the note after the list. Theme control sits in the header.

FORM: A bookmark register, fourth of seven grounded forms; seed 84436eb3. The list row itself is the signature interaction: entire codes remain obvious destinations while the destination stays readable. No decorative motion interferes with navigation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
